CaydenJoy Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · Applies to the CaydenJoy Android app and web app

In short: CaydenJoy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos, boards, settings and progress stay on your device. We do not run a server that receives or stores your data, and we never sell or share personal information.

Who we are

CaydenJoy is developed by Barney R. Gilliom (Gilliom Frontline Digital), United States. Contact: dallas8000@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

These items are created by you and saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Photos you add to boards (family, foods, places, objects) and their labels
  • Custom tabs, sentence-strip words and board choices
  • Accessibility and voice settings
  • The activity log shown on the Progress page
  • Which premium tier is unlocked on the device

Backups are only created when you tap Export Backup, which saves a file to your device. Where that file goes next is your choice.

Information we collect

None. CaydenJoy does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases

  • Google Play version: premium tiers are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The app checks your purchases with Google Play on the device to unlock features; we never receive your card or payment details.
  • Version downloaded from our website: payments are processed by Stripe under the Stripe Privacy Policy. We receive the purchase confirmation and the email address you give at checkout so we can deliver your upgrade key and provide support.

Network connections

All built-in pictures and symbols are included in the app, so boards work offline and the app does not contact any third-party servers while you use it. The Google Play version connects to Google Play only to show prices, make purchases and restore purchases. Speech uses your device's own text-to-speech voices.

Children

CaydenJoy is designed to be used by children with a parent, caregiver or therapist. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Photos a caregiver adds stay on the device. If you believe information about a child has reached us, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing photos in the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Uninstalling removes all app data, so export a backup first if you want to keep your photos. For the website version, you can ask us to delete purchase records by emailing us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Since we don't hold data from the app, most requests are satisfied by the controls above; for purchase records, email dallas8000@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
